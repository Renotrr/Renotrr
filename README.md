## How I approach automation

1. Understand the real workflow before choosing what to automate.
2. Map the data, states, handoffs, and failure points.
3. Use deterministic logic for rules and AI where unstructured information or language makes it useful.
4. Add validation, duplicate protection, useful logs, and retry handling.
5. Keep people involved where a decision needs context, approval, or accountability.

I’m interested in AI automation engineering, workflow integration, and product-building opportunities where I can help turn repetitive operational work into reliable systems.
